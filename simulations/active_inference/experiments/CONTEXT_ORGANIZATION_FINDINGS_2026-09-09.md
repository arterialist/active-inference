# Context-bound embodied learning, 9 September 2026

## Completed result

Eight acquisition/probe courses and four expression-lesion courses completed
across graph seeds 11, 23, 44 and 77: 81,536 executed neural/body ticks, including
the unchanged expression replays. This is a replicated partial capability and
a replicated failure, not an accepted complete memory system.

Contextual neural access protects earlier selected weights while the second
context is learned. The maximum change in the old bank is 2.66e-6 to 3.32e-6,
against changes of .125 to .206 in the newly active bank. In context-blind
acquisition, both banks change by .055 to .099. Preservation is not a learning
freeze: every basal rate stays positive and inactive inputs can lack eligible
events. These numbers describe the particular selected weight arrays, not a
percentage of semantic memory.

The protected weights have a useful causal contribution for recording 0 in
both contexts, which require opposite motor responses. In the first 32 probe
ticks, resetting acquired weights increases residual torque from .150–.158 to
.219–.221 Nm in the earlier context, and from .098–.099 to .171–.172 Nm in the
later context. The complete traces include later intervals where that ordering
changes. Removing context access during acquisition loses the early benefit
for the earlier-context recording-0 case, with .283–.286 Nm residual torque.
This is a whole-acquisition architectural comparison; body states are matched
between intact/reset probes within each architecture, not between architectures.

Recording 1 is worse with the learned state in the early interval, in both
contexts and all four seeds. The expression intervention explains why its
ordinary trajectory can nevertheless appear competent. Suppressing new neural
teaching arrivals leaves selected weight changes below 3.49e-8 while rates stay
at or above 1e-5. Existing error traces decay; weights are not explicitly frozen.
Recording-0 compensation remains, but recording-1 residual torque persists and
the joint moves far from its initial position. During ticks 256–299, peak
absolute angle is 1.48–1.73 rad in the earlier context and 1.40–1.64 rad in the
later context. With teaching intact these ranges are .323–.368 and .212–.254 rad.
The independent unlesioned replay matches every original probe array exactly
before each lesion is interpreted. The lesion also removes native retrograde
responses to the cleared teaching-port arrivals, so its effect is not attributed
to one isolated learning equation alone.

The finding is **protected context-dependent responses plus rapid online
correction, not reliable recall of the whole experienced repertoire**. Both
contexts ended acquisition with recording 0. Recency, overlapping sensory
features and the local learning rule remain competing explanations for the
recording-1 deficit. Four graph seeds do not counterbalance experience order.
Do not turn that unresolved distinction into a claim that one animal sound is
inherently harder to learn.

There is a task-design limitation too: force remains directly measurable.
Audiovisual memory is therefore not necessary for eventual compensation. This
preparation tests memory participation and interference, not the necessity of
prediction for survival. A useful next whole-agent challenge must give learned
relations an actual advantage over direct feedback, for example a consequential
interval before delayed force evidence becomes available. That does not require
zero latency or perfect movement. The next architecture should address usable
coexisting event representations and their authority over action, rather than
optimize this arm's endpoint or merely strengthen the context gate. Specialized
PAULA learning/dendritic mechanisms remain legitimate options; the current
positive-only estimator rule is not a limit of PAULA itself.

That observation is not permission to replace PAULA under an inherited class
name. First use the core's existing signed synaptic channels, parameterization
and composition. Any change to its defining dynamics needs a specific observed
limitation, a reason existing flexibility is insufficient, and a separate
testable justification. This experiment reuses existing opt-in mechanisms and
does not establish that a new neuron model is necessary.

All recorded selected-learning and physical-replay residuals are zero. The
80-probe comparison retains all body ticks and source hashes. Twenty-four
focused tests pass, including deliberate corruption and checkpoint continuation.
These checks do not reconstruct every upstream intracellular variable or prove
biological fidelity. No new neuron equation or shared model change was made.

## Question and declared experiment

Can one continually adapting PAULA network retain conflicting sensory-to-motor
relationships, with context controlling their expression? This follows the
metabolic preparation's failure to preserve useful movement despite preserving
rhythm. The objective is a composable adaptive organization, not better scalar
activity regulation. The broader mammal-level and consciousness goal remains
unachieved.

The new 590-neuron preparation receives the existing two real audiovisual
recordings through 96 pixel and 96 auditory channels. Two idealized context-lamp
photoreceptors indicate the current externally defined mechanical context.
The same recording accompanies a positive push in one context and a negative
push in the other. A single MuJoCo hinge responds to the applied torque and the
neural antagonist motor output. Opponent joint receptors provide a weak innate
restoring reflex. Applied-force receptors measure the imposed load, not a host
action target. The artificial experimental contingency is declared openly; it
is not a natural relationship between barking, chirping and mechanical force.

Two banks of 192 heterogeneous signed audiovisual neurons receive ordinary
inhibitory context projections. They supply two shared, locally plastic force
estimators. Actual force-receptor and estimator outputs drive opponent neural
comparison cells; their signals regulate the selected incoming weights through
the existing predictive-receptor extension. The estimator outputs reach the
muscles. No new neuron equation, host policy, semantic label, fitted readout or
learning freeze is introduced. Context-sensitive anatomy is specified, not
learned. Continuous sensory stimulation replaces the earlier four-phase input
schedule and is recorded explicitly.

Eight episodes in context 0 precede eight in context 1. Every episode has 300
stimulated ticks followed by 64 ticks without media or load. Context remains
visible. Each final learned whole state supplies eight matched physical probes:
two contexts by two recordings by intact versus selected-weight reset. Reset
touches acquired estimator weights only; it preserves ongoing learning,
membranes, traces and queued signals. Consequently later convergence of a reset
probe cannot be interpreted as memory-free performance. Separate context-blind
acquisition is the proposed architectural comparison, not an activity-matched
control. All unfavorable early intervals are retained.

## Interpretation limits

The load begins with the media. Successful estimation is not yet anticipation
of a future load. The stimulus library has two recordings with very different
auditory intensity; success would not establish semantic recognition or that
both senses are necessary. There is no autonomous inference of a hidden
context, learned supervisory policy, self-chosen goal or claim of consciousness.
The body is a physical one-joint preparation, not the previous rower or a
complete animal. Do not report its performance as an accepted V1–V4 result.

All cell display fields, selected weight/arrival/error/rate trajectories,
external input channels and complete physical integration states are recorded
per tick. Full neural checkpoints provide executable branch points. The
independent checks currently reconstruct selected plasticity and replay physics,
not every upstream intracellular mechanism. Exact branch-continuation and
deliberate-corruption tests pass. Those are instrument checks, not behavioral
acceptance.

## Research connection

Mante et al. studied context-dependent population computation in macaque PFC
and a trained recurrent model. Kim, Daie and Li found context-specific motor
preparatory representations during continual learning in mice. They motivate
testing context-bound learned organization; neither establishes the present
PAULA architecture or its local learning law. Biology is a source of hypotheses,
not an anatomical or timescale requirement for this experiment.

- [Mante et al., Nature 2013](https://www.nature.com/articles/nature12742)
- [Kim, Daie and Li, Nature 2025, author manuscript](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11735397/)

## Local reproduction

From the `active-inference` package, use fresh output names, never overwrite an
evidence directory. Repeat acquisition for seeds 11, 23, 44 and 77, with and
without `--blind`. The fixed source fixture paths and hashes are recorded in
each manifest. Raw audiovisual fixtures and executable checkpoints are local
research data and are not included in the source-only Git publication. A fresh
checkout therefore needs the same licensed media decoded by the existing
encoder, with hashes verified; source publication alone is not a claim that
those binary fixtures have been distributed.
