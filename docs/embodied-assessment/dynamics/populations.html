What it does not establish
Populations interacting is not enough to demonstrate hierarchical learning. A response to an incomplete cue could be caused by the initial wiring; a supervisor could respond to activity rather than surprise. The controls test these alternatives.
No simulator-calculated classification, phase estimate or prediction error enters the upper population or supervisors. All basal plasticity remains positive. The local plasticity-rate receptor is an explicitly experimental PAULA extension.
The missing tests include a learned action–perception contingency, generalization across input timings, and robust regulation across new seeds and sustained exposure. This preparation is not the full architecture from the notebook.




Recording, measurement and reproduction
The display keeps every tick but uses float32 presentation values. Research chunks retain float64 cellular observables, terminal output and, for the main run, every incoming information weight at every tick. Queues and all other plastic fields are not recorded every tick; the final full state, configuration, stimuli and source hashes are retained. Waveforms and template comparisons are read-only measurements.
Node placement is a fixed population layout. There is no fitted manifold, invented sine wave, brain-shaped mesh, or claim that this is biological EEG.
