The hierarchy is the experiment still to build.

A relation between sensory populations must become a usable associative state; that state must influence action; action must change the next sensory encounter; and neural regulation must preserve or revise the joint organization. Those loops are not present in this recording. Two cue labels are not evidence of multimodal association.

The decisive test is whether an upper regime still distinguishes the lower populations’ relationship when their individual activity distributions are matched, and whether cutting its descending influence removes recovery under a disturbance. A stack of plots, a common clock, or a stable-looking oscillation cannot answer that.
